The sound of Hollywood Joe is timeless and entertaining, and is full of good old Rock n' Roll as well as inspiring musicianship. He has lived in Memphis, St. Louis, as well as of course...Hollywood, California. And in his travels has picked up many, many influences. At present we don't know of his whereabouts, but was recently heard and seen playing in various groups and combos in the Rockabilly/Swing, Blues, and Jazz traditions. Anyone interested in hearing his sound, or purchasing the many of his CDs, can log on to: mp3.com , CD Baby , musicbuilder.com and many other music internet sites.
